Day 1 · Limits, Continuity & Theorems

Monday, May 11 · ~40 minutes · 13 randomized questions

Rebuild the foundation: factoring 0/0 limits, limits at infinity by degree comparison, classifying discontinuities (removable, jump, infinite), exact unit-circle values, and IVT/MVT setups. Biggest trap: writing the MVT citation completely — both hypotheses (continuous on [a,b] AND differentiable on (a,b)) AND the conclusion in interval notation.

Wednesday, May 13 · ~40 minutes · 13 randomized questions

First/second derivative tests for local extrema, L'Hôpital for 0/0 and ∞/∞ limits, related rates problems (ladder, balloon, square area, cone), concavity from f'', and inflection points. Required prep for tomorrow's FRQ work. The #1 related-rates trap is forgetting to multiply by dr/dt — the chain rule is what makes related rates "related."

Day 4 · Integration & Applications

Thursday, May 14 · ~40 minutes · 14 randomized questions

Antiderivatives (don't forget +C), trig antiderivatives, u-substitution including changing bounds on definite integrals, left/right/trapezoid Riemann sums and their over/under estimate behavior, FTC Part 1 with chain rule, integral additivity, and area between curves. Two free credit points to claim: always include +C on indefinite integrals, and always change bounds when u-subbing a definite integral.

AP Calculus AB · Cumulative Final Exam

Monday, May 18 · 35 questions · cumulative across all year

The final covers everything: limits and continuity, the IVT/MVT theorems, all differentiation rules, applications of derivatives (extrema, L'Hôpital, related rates, concavity), antiderivatives and u-substitution, Riemann sums, FTC, and applications of integration (area, displacement, physics). 29 multiple-choice questions (Q1–Q29), 1 multi-part numerical problem (Q30 · 4 sub-parts on particle motion), and 5 free-response problems (Q31–Q35).

What to expect

  • Each multiple-choice question has 6 options (A–F). Pick the single best.
  • Q30 has 4 typed numerical sub-parts (particle motion: t at rest, displacement, acceleration).
  • Q31–Q35 are typed free-response with multiple parts (related rates, MVT, area & volume, graph of f', Riemann/trapezoid sums).
